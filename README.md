<h2> About Me </h2>

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Facens, and I spend my days on the backend of radar systems as an NEA Engineering intern at Splice Group (Votorantim). In practice that means writing APIs in Go and C#, designing PostgreSQL schemas and migrations, and shipping services inside Docker containers.

What draws me to backend work is that it's where the real problems live: how data is modeled, how services talk to each other, and what happens when something fails at 3am. I also work with React on the frontend and build mobile apps in Java, which keeps me honest about the other side of the API.

These days I'm going deeper into Go and Java, studying networking and cybersecurity on the side, and pushing my English toward fluency.
